# MeBot 0.10.1

A redesigned Jobs workspace with guided, editable onboarding, clearer job cards and details, saved jobs, application notes and document drafts. Browse cached jobs and prepare applications without AI; connected AI is optional and shows what information will be shared before sending.

- Four-step role, skill and location setup, with examples and an explore-first option.
- Transparent matching reasons and eligibility cautions, not promises of qualification.
- Durable application notes and editable documents, PDF export and explicit review.
- Offline refresh queue and application tracking.

Install `mebot-0.10.1.apk` over the existing official MeBot app. Do not uninstall first. Android 8 or later is required. This update retains the production signing certificate and package identity.
