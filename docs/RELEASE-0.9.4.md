# MeBot 0.9.4 — your notes, a fresher feed

**In preparation. Not yet released.**

## What is improving
- After importing context, return to your details and see the notes you imported.
- Keep time-sensitive stories useful, while saving older articles and starred stories you still want.
- Match public stories more closely to your interests when your connected AI refreshes the feed.

Your saved items, starred stories and personal notes remain yours. AI is optional; cached reading and public sources continue to work without it.

Import still asks you to review before replacing your profile and context notes. An import with no module settings keeps your current module choices.

Production download and verification links will be added after the release checks finish. The current published release is [0.9.3](https://github.com/EmmanuelMmanda/MeBot/releases/tag/v0.9.3).
