What changed in 0.6.1
  • Find every moduleSearch, status/category filters, a disabled-module entry point, useful default context, and persistent ordering.
  • Control background usage28 as the new-profile starting limit, larger presets, a custom limit, Unlimited, and clear provider boundaries.
