What changed in 0.9.6

Questions stay connected to the story you chose while MeBot opens its conversation. You can start typing immediately; the draft stays in that story thread, and Send waits until the correct conversation is ready. Rotation and reopening return to the same story; app lock cancels pending sends and unlock restores the story without sending.

The 0.9.5 story-question experience is preserved, with the original publisher source still available. Technical evidence and device-test limits remain separate from these user-facing notes.

Earlier releases

MeBot 0.9.5 is superseded by 0.9.6 and remains available as an immutable historical signed release with its matching source. Scope warning: in 0.9.5, a question sent before the story conversation opens could land in a general chat instead of that story; wait until the story conversation is ready before sending. Versions 0.9.3 and earlier are also superseded; 0.9.0–0.9.2 had an external-launch defect. The 0.8.x package/signer lineage is separate from current 0.9.x updates. Versions 0.8.3–0.8.5 are historical source checkpoints without production APKs.
