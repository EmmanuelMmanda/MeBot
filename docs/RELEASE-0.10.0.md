# MeBot 0.10.0: Jobs workspace

**Released 2026-11-16.** [Official release and signed APK](https://github.com/EmmanuelMmanda/MeBot/releases/tag/v0.10.0). In MeBot 0.9.9, open You → Software updates to download the verified update. Do not uninstall to update.

The released source is `63e61dd8d8e149eb16c8c9428f5598e34822be9d`. The verification record below is a documentation-only follow-up; the release tag and signed assets remain immutable.

## Find and keep useful opportunities

Jobs has its own place in the main navigation. Set your roles, skills, country, work preferences and optional salary minimum, or skip setup and browse broadly. Matching runs on your device and does not need an AI connection.

Read attributed listings from Remotive and Jobicy, open the original source, and save opportunities for later. Matching explanations separate relevance from unknown employer eligibility. Explicit salary ranges can be compared within the same currency and compatible pay period; unknown pay remains visible rather than being guessed.

These are remote-job feeds, not a complete local or worldwide vacancy directory. Remotive listings are delayed by 24 hours; Jobicy lists a recent seven-day window with a three-hour delay. Refreshes are rate-limited and cached listings remain readable offline. With Tanzania selected, separate links open Work.tz and the official Ajira Portal. Those external listings are not included in MeBot matching or alerts.

## Keep applications together

Keep notes, an application stage and an editable document draft with each saved job. Opening a source link does not mark a job as applied. Saved snapshots remain readable when a listing leaves the current feed, with a reminder to verify availability at the source.

Jobs preferences and application records are included in personal-data exports. Import shows the effect before replacing records; imported alerts start off.

## Add AI when you choose

Open a conversation scoped to a job in the existing MeBot assistant. The normal streaming conversation and provider settings are reused. Jobs conversations do not include unrelated profile, module or feed context.

Bring a completed assistant answer into an editable application draft, review it and export a PDF. You remain responsible for confirming your evidence and submitting any application. PDF CV parsing and automatic submission are not included.

## Stay in control

Optional daily or weekly notifications use local matching, not paid background AI calls. Android notification permission and battery scheduling apply. Existing Feed, MeBot, Saved, You and all Modules remain available; Modules is reached from Feed's All modules action.

English and Kiswahili copy, current themes and accent choices are preserved.
