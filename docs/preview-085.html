Make MeBot yours with less setup

01

Your interests, in your words

Onboarding lets you add custom interests as well as select topics. A live count helps you stay within 150 words and 1,000 characters. Words are separated by whitespace, including Unicode whitespace. Both limits apply; the app does not silently shorten your text.

Your custom interests are saved with explicit topic scope. They are not automatically shared with every module.

02

One place to introduce yourself

About you has one primary text area for your work, interests and what you want to follow. Write only what is useful. Optional Help shows an example in English or Kiswahili; it is not inserted or saved automatically.

Choose to insert an example yourself. If you already wrote something, confirm before replacing it. Your earlier individual context records remain intact under collapsed Saved details; simplifying the screen does not discard them.

Location, time zone and import/export options are grouped under Location, time zone & transfer, while Save details stays prominent. Check your changes before saving.

03

Find topics and saved reading more easily

Modules uses a compact search field and horizontal filter rows, leaving more room for topics. Saved and Following have a clearer hierarchy: bookmarks keep reading, while stars follow stories. Manage follow-up timing in Saved → Following.

The price-comparison tool has been removed from 0.8.5. An older saved route to that tool returns safely to Modules. Other everyday tools remain available.

Before a signed release

The local gate passed 182 core, 490 Android and 169 service tests, with zero failures, errors or skips. Release assembly and ZIP alignment passed. Lint reported zero errors, 221 debug warnings, 222 release warnings and one hint per variant.

If the same production package uses a new signing certificate, it cannot update the old 0.8.2 installation in place. Export portable preferences before any owner-controlled reinstall; this does not promise that every kind of app data can be restored. MeBot will not automatically uninstall your existing app.

What stays the same

The icon-only star beside the bookmark, source-dependent fullscreen media, optional notifications and normal request allowances remain. Provider-supported controls do not guarantee that every video can play. Device speech input and installed-voice read-aloud are not full-duplex realtime voice.

Native captures use controlled example content. They show the interface, not live provider authorization, physical-phone installation, real streaming, notification delivery or overnight alarms.
