MeBot 0.9.6 · story-conversation update

Keep your question with its story.

Open a story and start typing right away. MeBot keeps the question attached to that story while its conversation prepares; sending remains your choice.

One story, one conversation

  1. Open a story. Your conversation stays connected to the story you chose.
  2. Start your question. Your draft stays with that story while MeBot opens the right conversation. Send waits until it is ready.
  3. Return without losing context. Rotation or reopening restores the same story conversation. Locking stops a pending question; unlocking brings the story back without sending it.

Available in the official release

MeBot 0.9.6 is the official Android release. Install over an existing production 0.9.x app without uninstalling or clearing data.
