# MeBot 0.9.6 — keep your question with its story

- Keep a question connected to the story you opened, even when you start typing immediately.
- Keep your draft while MeBot prepares the conversation, instead of sending it to a general chat.
- Return to the right story conversation after rotating or reopening the app.
- Locking the app stops a pending question from being sent. Unlocking brings back the story without sending it for you.

Original sources remain available. You decide when to send.

Update the existing production MeBot 0.9.x app without uninstalling or clearing data. Older, differently signed apps are a separate installation lineage. Technical verification and device-test limits are in the accompanying verification report.
