# MeBot 0.9.3 — file selection and notification fixes

[Download MeBot 0.9.3](https://github.com/EmmanuelMmanda/MeBot/releases/tag/v0.9.3) · Released 26 October 2026

## What’s improved

- **Choose a context file without the app closing.** You can still paste your context instead, then review it before saving.
- **Export your data without the app closing when the save dialog opens.**
- **Turn on notifications without the app closing.** The setting now says **Notify me about new updates** / **Nipe arifa za taarifa mpya**, so it is easier to distinguish notifications from background refresh.

Your familiar layout, interests, notes, saved content and app-lock settings stay in place.

## Known issue

After confirming a context import, the app can stay on the import screen instead of returning to your notes. A follow-up correction is being tested. Do not uninstall the app or clear its data to resolve this.

## Updating

Install this update over MeBot 0.9.0, 0.9.1 or 0.9.2. **Do not uninstall or clear your data.** Those versions can close during file selection or notification setup; use 0.9.3 instead.

Older 0.8.x installations use a separate app identity and cannot be updated in place with this release. See the [installation guide](INSTALL.md).
