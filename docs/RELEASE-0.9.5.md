# MeBot 0.9.5

In preparation. The current public update is [0.9.4](https://github.com/EmmanuelMmanda/MeBot/releases/tag/v0.9.4).

## A more useful next step

- Open a story in MeBot first, with its publisher still clearly shown.
- Recognize the type of story from a small category label rather than an extra button.
- Explore suggested questions about what you are reading, in your feed, story detail and MeBot conversation.
- Review or edit a suggested question before sending it. Choosing one does not send a request automatically.
- Open the original source directly from story details or the card menu whenever you want.

When a connected AI has supplied suitable questions, MeBot can reuse them for that story. Simple local questions remain available without a connection. Suggestions do not replace the source or promise that an answer is already verified.

Your existing notes, saved and starred stories, media controls and app lock remain part of the app. This version is intended as an in-place update for the existing 0.9.x production app; do not uninstall or clear your data to update.

Technical checks and remaining device-testing limits will be recorded separately before release.
