A short walk through MeBot

The important actions, with sample app screens. Move at your own pace.

First launch

Choose your interests

Pick topics, not technical settings.

Choose topics and an optional city. Skip anything you do not need. Onboarding preferences personalize your feed; a JSON import is optional.

You → My context

Import only what you want

Review first, then see your notes.

Review the replacement before saving. After confirmation, return to your details and see the imported notes. Cancel or correct a file without losing your draft.

Detailed guide →

Feed → story details → MeBot

Ask about the story you opened

Keep the next question grounded.

Read with the publisher visible, open the original source whenever you want, and choose an item-specific question in Feed or story details. MeBot prepares an editable draft; you decide whether to send it. Local questions remain available offline, and Relevant / Not relevant still tunes future results.

Detailed guide →

You → AI connection

Connect your provider

Choose the account or server you use.

ChatGPT and OpenRouter have different authorization flows. Claude and Gemini use API keys. A compatible personal server is explicit. The screen shows the actual capabilities and local request allowance.

Detailed guide →

My MeBot → background settings

Set your pace

Decide what happens in the background.

Choose intervals, module preparation, network conditions and quiet hours. Usage limits are managed in AI connection. Completed modules publish without waiting for the rest.

Detailed guide →

You → Software Updates

Keep MeBot current

Verify, then install.

Automatic checks find eligible releases for this package. Download verification checks integrity and signing. Android asks you to approve installation; your current app is not silently replaced.

Modules → daily tools

Use the tools you need

Alarm, Focus and Check-in.

Configure alarms, start a focus session or make a short check-in. These remain optional and work independently of the AI connection. Test alarm sound on your device.
