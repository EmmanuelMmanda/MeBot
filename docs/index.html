Story questions

Ask your selected AI about a story.

With an AI provider connected, open the story’s question sheet. Choose Send to ask a question, or Edit to change the draft first. Opening the sheet alone sends nothing.

Example questions for FSDT grants for eligible small businesses. Suggestions depend on that story and the AI connection you choose.

  1. 01Which FSDT eligibility rule should a digital-solutions business check before drafting an application?
  2. 02What records could help document the ownership or leadership criteria? Check the exact evidence required in FSDT’s application form.
  3. 03How could a small team connect its digital solution to one of FSDT’s listed priority sectors?
  4. 04Which costs and outcomes should a business clarify against the grant ceiling of up to TZS 30 million?
  5. 05What evidence would show that a proposed project can deliver measurable value without exceeding the call’s scope?
