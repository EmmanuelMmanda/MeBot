Feed, stories & AI

Start with a short feed, open one story for more detail, and keep the conversation connected to what you are reading.

Read and refresh the feed#

Feed starts with five stories. Use the module filters to narrow it and Show more to reveal the next cached items. Local filtering and pagination do not spend AI requests. Tap Feed while already there to return to the top and refresh.

Available stories are ordered using relevance, freshness, module and publisher variety, useful deadlines, and optional reading history. Your approved context and module preferences guide discovery. Turning on more modules does not guarantee a new suitable story in every refresh.

Save a useful story to return to it in Saved. Saved items remain when you pause a module or remove a custom Watchlist. You can hide an unwanted story and restore hidden updates from You → Data and backups.

Open a story#

A story brings together the overview, original source, dates, media when available, and useful fields such as Key points, Useful next step, or What to check. Those fields depend on the supported source information; they are not fabricated to fill a template.

With connected AI, A closer look starts automatically. ChatGPT and direct API routes stream the answer. A personal service returns a completed response. The request includes the story and only that module kind’s approved context.

A completed explanation can be reused for six hours when its story, settings, context, and connection still match. An interrupted partial answer is not saved as a completed explanation. If an attempt fails, the story remains available and you can retry.

Source links remain available for checking requirements, dates, quotes, or any uncertainty. Factual main links must be backed by provider source/citation metadata; that check does not prove every sentence of an AI answer.

Ask a useful follow-up#

Use the story’s question action or a relevant starting point, such as checking eligibility, finding a fix, or comparing options. MeBot continues with that story’s context rather than making you retype the whole item.

Follow-ups still use your selected AI route and request allowance. A source that is unavailable, a paywall, or missing evidence can limit the answer. MeBot does not provide paywall bypass or guarantee a full copy of a publisher’s article.

Search across your modules#

  1. Open Search from Feed and enter a keyword or question.
  2. Search the selected enabled factual modules, or choose one module.
  3. Submit the query. MeBot uses one AI attempt and returns up to eight supported results without replacing your daily feed.

Search requires a working AI connection and at least one enabled supported factual module. Weather and Horoscope use their dedicated flows. No matching supported result is a valid empty state; try a clearer topic or adjust the module selection.

Use the AI workspace#

AI supports saved threads and source-linked answers. A general thread receives its conversation and text you explicitly attach, without saved personal notes. Starting from a module or story permits only that kind’s confirmed context.

The app keeps up to 24 threads, 60 turns per thread, and 800,000 combined text characters. Requests use a bounded recent window of up to 16 turns and 36,000 characters; seeing an older message locally does not mean it is resent every time.

Attach up to three UTF-8 .txt or .md files, at most 48 KiB per file and 24,000 text characters combined. Review and remove attachments before sending. PDFs, images, and audio are not supported conversation attachments in this release.

Compatible Android voice input fills the editable draft without sending it automatically. Read-aloud uses the phone’s speech service. Its network behavior depends on that service; these features are separate from subscription audio APIs. MeBot does not keep an audio recording.

Media, offline reading, and limits#

Publisher images and direct media/source links appear when available. MeBot is not a video-hosting or download service. Cached text can remain useful even if an image or source page is unavailable.

Saved stories, cached feed text, completed explanations, conversations, and local tools remain readable offline. New answers, search, live forecasts, and uncached images require connectivity. A failed refresh preserves available old content; check dates before treating it as current.

For limits and backup options, see Background & your data.
