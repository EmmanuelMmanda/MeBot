Privacy & data boundaries

MeBot stores its reading state and everyday tools locally. AI features send only the data needed for the request through the connection you choose.

What stays on the phone#

The feed cache, saved stories, context notes, conversations, reading history, preferences, and local-tool records are stored on the device. Credentials are kept in an Android Keystore-backed encrypted vault and are excluded from portable backups.

Alarm scheduling and active ringing use a separate device-protected store for operational state needed after reboot. Alarms and local tools do not depend on AI or the feed background scheduler.

Android automatic app backup and device transfer are disabled. There is no built-in cloud sync, remote recovery service, advertising SDK, or analytics tracker in this release.

What is sent for an AI request#

RequestRelevant content
Module discoveryModule kind, default editorial guidance, topic/preferences, language/place/timezone as needed, and confirmed notes approved for that kind.
Story explanation or follow-upThe selected story and its source information, request/conversation text, relevant settings, and that kind’s approved context.
SearchYour query, selected factual modules, their preferences/defaults, and permitted context.
General conversationThe bounded recent conversation and explicitly attached text. No saved context notes until you select a module/story scope.

Direct API and ChatGPT requests go to OpenAI through their separate credential routes. With My service, request content also reaches your configured server and its provider. The operator’s logging and retention settings matter.

Scoped context limits what MeBot submits; it is not a provider-retention guarantee. Review the terms and data controls of the connection you choose. Treat an attachment or conversation as information you are deliberately sending.

What ChatGPT sign-in does not expose#

Official sign-in verifies your account and authorizes eligible inference use through a separate consent. It does not provide your ChatGPT conversations, saved memory, private connectors, or another person’s information.

The subscription integration also does not supply image-generation/audio/video/Files APIs or hosted MCP tools. Web-search availability is conditional. See OpenAI’s quickstart and current limitations.

Prepare in ChatGPT is a user-controlled transfer: it copies a generic prompt, opens ChatGPT, and asks you to review returned JSON before import. MeBot cannot silently synchronize memory through that flow.

Public sources, media, and phone services#

Public retrieval and publisher image loading contact the relevant source hosts. They can observe ordinary network information such as the requesting IP address. Images and links are not evidence of a private account connection.

Weather uses the place you enter, not GPS access. QR scanning is local. Selected alarm audio uses a document read grant; MeBot does not copy the audio bytes into a backup or gain access to the entire library.

General voice input and read-aloud use Android’s chosen speech services, whose network behavior depends on the installed service. Alarm speech requires an offline voice and does not call connected AI. MeBot does not retain an audio recording.

Exports and deletion#

JSON exports omit credentials but may contain personal notes, conversations, attachments, history, or alarm checkpoint values. They are not encrypted. A selected cloud document destination may upload them under that provider’s settings.

Context import, feed restore, and local-tool restore have different replacement scopes. Read the backup comparison before importing. Restoring a full feed backup clears AI credentials and leaves delivery off; imported local alarms start disabled.

Local erasure removes MeBot’s local state, credentials, work, and alarms after confirmation. It does not delete existing exports, source audio files, provider-side records, or a personal-service database. It does not guarantee forensic secure erasure.

This documentation website#

The website serves static HTML, CSS, JavaScript, fonts, and screenshots from its host. It has no analytics, third-party scripts, external font CDN, sign-in form, or embedded live app account.

The appearance selector stores only mebot.docs.theme in your browser’s local storage. Guide search runs locally in the page. Download availability is read from the site’s own release metadata; it does not inspect your device or account. The hosting provider may retain ordinary access logs under its own policy.

External links open the original documentation, publisher, or GitHub page you select. Copy buttons place only the displayed example prompt or command on your clipboard.

About the native screenshots#

These are real MeBot Compose screens rendered by Android host tests, using synthetic accounts and content. They show native app layout and controls, including narrow screens and larger text, without exposing a real user’s profile.

They are not generated app mockups, live provider results, or physical-phone reliability evidence. Some images first captured for an earlier version illustrate a workflow retained in 0.6.1. The screenshot notes and verification record describe the test boundaries.

The site icon follows MeBot’s native vector mark. Figtree is hosted locally under its font license. Source and third-party notices are in NOTICE.
