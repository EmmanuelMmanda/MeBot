MeBot 0.8.0 guide

Make MeBot look like you

Choose your colours and page style.

Choose an accent

Open You → Preferences → Appearance. Choose one of eight colours or tap Custom and enter a six-digit colour such as #7251B5. Apply saves your choice; invalid input cannot be applied.

Change the page style

Original keeps the standard surfaces. Warm softens the background. Soft colour adds a light tint. Deep black is available for a darker presentation in dark mode.

Light, dark and device modes are separate from your accent. Foreground colours are adjusted for readability without overwriting the colour you saved.

Your content stays yours

Appearance changes do not delete stories, alter your AI account or reset alarms. You can return to the original look at any time.
