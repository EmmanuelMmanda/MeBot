MeBot 0.9.8 guide

Save files and open media

Keep useful resources without interrupting your AI answer.

Save a file from an answer

Open Files in the answer’s resource area. When the original publisher provides a supported public file, tap Save file. After MeBot checks the download, Android asks where you want to save it.

Supported types include PDF, text, CSV, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX and common images. The limit is 20 MiB per file. The source must provide a genuine public HTTPS file; MeBot does not invent a PDF or bypass sign-in.

Open media when you need it

Choose Media to see available image, audio or video resources. Players start after a tap. Only one preview is expanded at a time, and Open original remains available when a provider cannot play inside the app.

External players may show their own buffering controls or restrictions. A preview or download failure does not replace a completed AI answer.

Keep the original story

The story headline, explanation, source, save/share actions and follow-up remain available. Expand original notes for the full saved summary and suggested next steps.
