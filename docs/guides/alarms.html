Alarms & everyday tools

Set an alarm that suits your morning. Local alarms, Focus, and check-in work independently of your AI connection.

Create or edit an alarm#

  1. Open Alarms, then add a plan or select an existing one.
  2. Choose the time, label, and repeating weekdays. With no weekdays selected, the alarm rings at the next occurrence only.
  3. Under Turn off with, choose Math, QR scan, or A tap.
  4. Open Sound & snooze to choose audio, volume, gradual start, vibration, and snooze rules.
  5. Save, complete any required phone-access setup, and test the alarm on your actual phone.

New alarms start with three easy math questions and Pulse. Existing alarms preserve their previous settings.

Choose a sound#

In Sound & snooze, select Pulse, Rising chime, Steady bell, or Classic. These are bundled tones and do not need a network.

Phone alarm sounds opens Android’s system picker. Choose audio file opens the document picker so you can select your own audio. MeBot keeps read access to that file, not a copy of its bytes or access to your whole music library.

Preview sound plays for up to 12 seconds at the phone’s current alarm volume. It stops on leaving the editor, going into the background, changing the sound, or an actual alarm taking over. Preview does not change the system volume or demonstrate the saved gradual ramp.

If selected audio is deleted, inaccessible, or unavailable before first unlock after a restart, ringing falls back to bundled Pulse. Old saved plans with no new audio selection keep Classic. Prefer a local file and test the sound route you intend to use.

Add a spoken wake-up message#

Enable Spoken wake-up message. Leave the text empty for the localized default or enter your own message, up to 240 characters.

The feature uses an installed offline Android voice in the selected language. Scheduled speech starts after the saved volume ramp, with a minimum delay of 1.5 seconds. Preview demonstrates it after about one second. If an offline voice is unavailable, the tone continues.

No AI request is used for alarm speech. Installing a suitable offline voice may require the phone’s speech settings; MeBot cannot guarantee a voice engine is present on every device.

Challenges, snooze, and volume#

ControlAvailable choices
MathOne to five questions, Easy / Medium / Hard. Questions are generated locally; a wrong answer does not dismiss the alarm.
QR scanCreate a checkpoint code, give it a place label, and save its image. Optional follow-up: off, 1, 3, or 5 minutes.
A tapDismiss directly from the ringing screen.
Snoozes allowedZero to three. If enabled, choose 1, 3, 5, or 10 minutes per snooze.
Saved alarm volume10%–100%. New alarms use 80%; Gradual volume offers 0, 10, 20, or 30 seconds.

Math progress and snooze state belong to the current alarm occurrence. QR scanning is local and uses camera permission. Place the checkpoint somewhere useful, but do not treat it as proof of location or wakefulness.

The ringing screen always keeps an emergency-stop path with confirmation, including when a challenge cannot be completed.

Set up and test your phone#

Alarm setup links to Exact timing, Notifications, and Lock screen access, plus Android background battery settings. If required access is missing, use the explicit option to save the plan switched off while you complete setup.

Below Save alarm, choose Test in 10 seconds for a short real test. Check the locked screen, intended Bluetooth/headphone or speaker route, and behavior after reboot. Preview alone does not test scheduled delivery.

The phone must be on. Do Not Disturb, stream volume, permissions, and manufacturer sleep restrictions can affect ringing. Force-stopping MeBot can prevent alarms until it is reopened; closing the visible app is different. Recheck custom audio after moving a backup to another device.

Host tests cover software state and scheduling behavior, not overnight reliability on every phone. See the device acceptance checks.

Focus, check-in, and history#

Focus supports a timed session, pause/resume, and completion. It does not play a background completion alarm in this release. Daily check-in lets you record a next useful step and optional mood or notes. It does not diagnose or score your mental health.

History returns to saved plans and sessions. Tool toggles affect availability without deleting records. These local records are separate from feed context and are not automatically included in AI requests.
